Diversi Presenti

2024
  • Milan, Società Umanitaria
  • Curated by Stefano Serretta
  • With Stefano Boccalini and Andrea Tinterri

The exhibition is the outcome of the work carried out during the first semester of the 2023/24 academic year by the students of the Three-Year Bachelor’s Degree in Painting and Visual Arts at NABA, Nuova Accademia di Belle Arti, in collaboration with Società Umanitaria and under the guidance of Stefano Boccalini and Stefano Serretta. The title of the exhibition refers to Philip K. Dick: Many claim to remember a past life, but I claim to remember another, very different, present life . The quotation by the writer who became a cult figure in science fiction serves as a starting point for highlighting the heterogeneity of the vision of the Italian and international students of NABA’s Three-Year Bachelor’s Degree. The students reflected on the expressive possibilities of various artistic practices to prefigure a concept such as the future, increasingly compressed into an eternal present, at a time when real time and real-time become confused, often cancelling each other out, just as the differences between physical and virtual space are blurred. The students worked through site-specific approaches, imagining works capable of creating a direct dialogue with the spaces of Società Umanitaria (architecturally distinctive spaces, far removed from the white cube in which contemporary art is often confined), and did not shy away from engaging with new technologies and expressive possibilities, such as IA and video and digital processing, alongside painting, sculpture and performance.
