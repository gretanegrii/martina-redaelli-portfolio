Tumbleweed. Quando le mappe non creano imperi

2026
  • Milan, Società Umanitaria
  • Curated by Andrea Tinterri and Agata Rubyte

The archive is a mobile body that questions the present, susceptible to history and, above all, to anyone who approaches it or plunges into it for an immersion, a baptism, a prolonged apnea. But the archive is also, above all for the reasons just described, a laboratory in which memory can be re-signified and history can become a pretext for new trajectories. And it was precisely this laboratory-like character that directed the research of the students of NABA’s Three-Year Bachelor’s Degree in Painting and Visual Arts, guided by lecturers Stefano Boccalini and Stefano Serretta, into the history of Società Umanitaria [read more on umanitaria.it]
