Concrete Forest

2025
  • Installation series
  • Digital photograph on laser-cut plexiglass
  • 55 × 59 × 0,3 cm; 55 × 40 × 0,3 cm; 55 × 36 × 0,3 cm

Concrete Forest 2025 stems from a personal confrontation with the suffocating presence of the city of Milan, where the vastness of the urban landscape turns into a cage and space itself seems to offer no possibility of escape.

The city's boundaries appear to stretch endlessly, not only horizontally but also vertically: skyscrapers and overhead wires fragment the sky, undermining the idea of open space and transforming it into yet another boundary.

The work functions as an escape mechanism—an attempt to imagine a way out of the urban labyrinth that surrounds me.
