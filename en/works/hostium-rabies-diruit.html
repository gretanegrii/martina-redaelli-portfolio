Hostium Rabies Diruit — La rabbia dei nemici distrusse

2024
  • 4 stamps, 3,5 × 3 cm
  • Print on sticker

During the Italian Social Republic, from 1944 until 1945, 2 series of stamps were issued in Italy called "series of destroyed monuments". On the stamps were some Italian monuments destroyed by Allied bombing during the Second World War, alongside the symbols of fascist propaganda. On all the stamps was present the Latin word “Hostium Rabies Diruit”, whose meaning is: "The anger of the enemies destroyed."

The 4 stamps I made make up a sort of continuation, a "third series" of Monuments Stamps destroyed. This time I chose as subjects some modern buildings of the city of Milan: the skyscrapers of CityLife, the Torre Velasca, the Torre Unicredit and the Pirelli Skyscraper. These four images are also computer-made collages printed like real sticky stamps, 3.5 × 3 cm.

Exhibited at Diversi Presenti (2024), Società Umanitaria, Milan.
