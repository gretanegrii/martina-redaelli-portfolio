The Second Salary

2024
  • Found objects
  • 52 × 25 cm

The Second Salary 2024 was developed alongside the demolition of the former Cagi factory, one of the historic buildings in Motta Visconti, which for decades occupied the centre of the town. After the factory closed, the building remained abandoned for years, becoming a silent yet constant presence in the urban landscape and in the town's collective memory. Cagi played a significant role in the local economy and, above all, in the emancipation of women: the majority of its workforce consisted of women, whose wages often represented a second income that was essential to their families' livelihoods.

Before its complete demolition, and the construction of a supermarket in its place, I had the opportunity to visit the factory one last time and collect fragments from the site, preserving them as material traces of its history. Alongside this process, I conducted a series of interviews with former Cagi workers from my town, who shared memories of their years at the factory and reflected on the significance that work had in their lives. The project reflects on the role of labour in women's emancipation and in supporting family well-being, while also addressing the decline of Italian manufacturing, the relocation of production to countries with lower labour costs, and the abandonment of industrial sites, which gradually become what Gilles Clément defines as the Third Landscape.

The work is accompanied by research and interviews collected in the essay The Second Salary.
