Postcards They Won’t Receive

2025
  • Series of 3 postcards
  • Digital print on postcard, 105 × 148 mm
  • Urban Intervention

What does it mean to be homeless in a society where housing is a central element of identity? What does it mean to survive in a city that ignores you?

Postcards They Won’t Receive is a series of 3 postcards conceived as a tool of urban counter-narration of Milan Through the overlay of two registers, the celebratory language of real estate and concrete data on homelessness, it exposes the contradiction between the rhetoric of growth and that of exclusion.

The three selected locations are among the construction sites in Milan investigated in July 2025 for irregularities and speculative practices. The format, traditionally associated with tourism, serves as a medium through which the city sells an idealized and polished image of itself. The “postcards” celebrate large construction sites as symbols of growth while simultaneously revealing what lies beneath their scaffolding.

When art is instrumentalized to support mechanisms that exclude and oppress the less privileged, can it still be considered art, or does it become merely a form of creative publicity disguised as artistic expression?

The postcards draw on the field research gathered in the essay Homelessness in Milan: Losing One’s Home and Identity.
