Ruins

2024
  • UV print on Forex board, 100 × 70 cm

My vision of the future takes the form of a dystopia that feels unsettlingly close to the present. Created for the exhibition Diversi Presenti 2024, the work stems from an observation of contemporary conflicts and the images of destruction that continuously shape our everyday reality.

By imagining a future marked by ruins, the work reflects on the material and symbolic value of places that embody collective memory. The Duomo di Milano becomes the central subject of the piece, depicted as a heap of rubble, questioning the apparent permanence of monuments and revealing the fragility of the symbols that define our cultural landscape.

The project draws inspiration from the Montagnetta di San Siro, an artificial hill built from the debris of the Second World War and later transformed into a public green space. Here, ruins become not only a testimony to loss and destruction but also a site of transformation and renewal.

The work invites viewers to reflect on the relationship between present and future, considering both the consequences of human actions and the possibility of imagining alternative futures before irreversible loss occurs.

Exhibited at Diversi Presenti (2024), Società Umanitaria, Milan.
