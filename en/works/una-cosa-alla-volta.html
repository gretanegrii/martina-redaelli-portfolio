One Thing At Time

2024
  • 4′18″
  • Short film made in collaboration with Giorgia Checcucci

Bea is twenty years old and finds herself suspended in that undefined space between what she was and what she is supposed to become. It is the moment when paths seem endless, yet none of them clear enough to be followed. It is the Quarter Life Crisis: a fragile time made of unanswered questions, expectations that are difficult to carry, and the invisible weight of the future.

After moving to Milan in search of a new direction, Bea finds herself alone with herself. One evening, closed within the walls of her home, silence gives way to the noise of her thoughts: fears, desires, memories, and insecurities intertwine in an inner dialogue that becomes the portrait of a generation searching for its place in the world.

Through her vulnerability, the short film tells the delicate transition into adulthood, that moment when we learn that growing up does not mean having all the answers, but accepting uncertainty and finding the courage to move through it.
